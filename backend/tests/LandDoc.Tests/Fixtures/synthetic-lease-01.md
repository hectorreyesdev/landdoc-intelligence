# Oil and Gas Lease

**Lessor:** John Q. Landowner
**Lessee:** Acme Minerals LLC
**Effective Date:** January 15, 2026

## Legal Description

Section 14, Block 2, Township 1 North, Permian County, Texas.

## Royalty

The royalty reserved by Lessor shall be three-sixteenths (3/16) of all oil and gas produced and saved
from the above-described land.

## Primary Term

This lease shall be for a primary term of three (3) years from the Effective Date, and as long thereafter
as oil or gas is produced in paying quantities from the leased premises.

## Delay Rentals

This is a paid-up lease. No delay rentals are required during the primary term.

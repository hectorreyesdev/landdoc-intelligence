# Drilling Title Opinion

TO: Delaware Basin Resources, LP

FROM: T. Lindqvist, Attorney at Law, Lindqvist & Reyes PLLC

DATE: April 28, 2025

RE: Section 30, Block C-24, PSL Survey, Abstract No. 612, Loving County, Texas (640.00 acres)


## Scope of Examination

At your request, I have examined an abstract of title covering the captioned lands, comprising 63 numbered entries certified to March 31, 2025 by Trans-Pecos Abstract Co.. This opinion is limited to record title and is rendered for the sole use of the addressee.

## Marketable Title

Subject to the comments and requirements below, marketable title to the mineral estate is vested as follows: Mentone Minerals, Ltd. (undivided 7/8) owns the executive mineral interest, subject to a non-participating royalty of 1/8 of 8/8 held by the Henderson Family.

## Comments

Comment 1: The 1987 probate of the Estate of A. Henderson did not include a recorded order admitting the will; heirship is presumed from the family settlement agreement at Book 412, Page 88.

Comment 2: A prior oil and gas lease appears expired by its own terms for lack of production but no release of record was located.

## Requirements

Requirement 1: Obtain and record a release of the expired lease referenced in Comment 2, or a stipulation of interest and non-development.

Requirement 2: Secure recorded affidavits of heirship for the Henderson interest before disbursing proceeds; suspend the corresponding decimal interest until cured.

Requirement 3: Confirm payment of ad valorem taxes for the current year; unpaid taxes constitute a first lien on the property.

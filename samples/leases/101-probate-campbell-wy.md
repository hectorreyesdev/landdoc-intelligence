IN THE PROBATE COURT

CAMPBELL COUNTY, WYOMING


IN THE MATTER OF THE ESTATE OF WALTER P. REAVES, DECEASED.

No. P-1101


# Order Admitting Will to Probate and Appointing Executor

On December 5, 2024 came on to be heard the application for probate of the written will of Walter P. Reaves, Deceased, and the Court, having heard the evidence, FINDS:

1. The Decedent died on June 21, 2024, and this Court has jurisdiction and venue. 2. The Decedent left a valid written will dated October 17, 2024, not revoked. 3. Carl Reaves is named executor in the will and is not disqualified.

IT IS THEREFORE ORDERED that the will is admitted to probate; that Carl Reaves is appointed and shall serve as independent executor without bond; and that letters testamentary issue upon qualification according to law.

The Decedent's estate includes, among other property, an interest in the oil, gas, and minerals described as Township 50 North, Range 68 West, 6th P.M., Section 22: S/2, Campbell County, Wyoming.


SIGNED this December 5, 2024.

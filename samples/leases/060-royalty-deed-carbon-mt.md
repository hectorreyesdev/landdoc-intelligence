# Royalty Deed

THIS ROYALTY DEED is made February 14, 2025 by Cuero Creek Ranch, Ltd. ("Grantor") to Santa Rita Royalty Company ("Grantee").

## Granting Clause

For valuable consideration of $48,000.00, Grantor grants and conveys unto Grantee a perpetual, non-participating royalty interest equal to an undivided 1/8 of all oil, gas, and other minerals produced, saved, and sold from the land described below, free and clear of all costs of exploration, development, and production.

## Land

Township 7 South, Range 20 East, M.P.M., Section 21: NE/4, containing 160.00 acres, more or less, Carbon County, Montana.

## Non-Participating Nature

The interest conveyed is a non-participating royalty: Grantee shall not have the right to execute leases, to receive bonus or delay rentals, or to join in the making of oil and gas leases, all such executive rights being reserved to the mineral owner.

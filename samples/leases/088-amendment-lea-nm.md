# Amendment and Extension of Oil and Gas Lease

This Amendment and Extension ("Amendment") is made October 17, 2024 between Estate of Harlan W. Dietrich, by Susan Dietrich, Personal Representative ("Lessor") and Cimarron Operating, LLC ("Lessee").

## Original Lease

Lessor and Lessee are parties to that certain Oil and Gas Lease dated January 12, 2024, recorded as 2022-0005664 in Book 1316, Page 983 of the records of Lea County, New Mexico (the "Lease"), covering: Township 16 South, Range 35 East, N.M.P.M., Section 4: SE/4, 160.00 acres.

## Extension of Primary Term

In consideration of $2,750.00 per net acre, the primary term of the Lease is extended for an additional period of three (3) years from the expiration of the original primary term, upon the same terms except as amended below.

## Amended Royalty

The royalty under the Lease is amended and increased to three-sixteenths (3/16) of production, effective as to production on and after the date of this Amendment.

## Ratification

Except as expressly amended, all terms of the Lease are ratified and confirmed and remain in full force and effect. Lessor ratifies and confirms the Lease as amended.

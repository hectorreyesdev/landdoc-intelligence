# Oil and Gas Lease (Appalachian Form)

This Lease, made and entered into on October 1, 2024, between Robert E. Stanton and Patricia Stanton, his wife, of Amwell Township, Washington County, Pennsylvania ("Lessor"), and Keystone Shale Partners, LP ("Lessee").


## Witnesseth

That for and in consideration of $3,000.00 per net acre and the covenants hereinafter contained, Lessor does hereby grant, demise, lease, and let unto Lessee, for the sole and only purpose of exploring for and producing oil and gas, all that certain tract of land described as follows:

## Description (Metes and Bounds)

BEGINNING at an iron pin at the northwest corner of lands now or formerly of Reynolds; thence S 82 deg E 1,420 feet to a post; thence S 6 deg W 3,160 feet along lands of Maple Run to a stone; thence N 84 deg W 1,390 feet to a white oak; thence N 5 deg E 3,090 feet to the place of beginning

Containing 112.40 acres, more or less, being the same premises described in Deed Book 1123, Page 456, situate in Amwell Township, Washington County, Pennsylvania.

## Habendum / Term

To have and to hold for a term of five (5) years from the date hereof (the primary term) and so long thereafter as oil or gas is produced in paying quantities.

## Royalty

Lessee covenants to pay Lessor a royalty of one-eighth (1/8) of the net proceeds at the wellhead for all gas produced and marketed, and one-eighth (1/8) of all oil produced and saved from the leased premises.

## Delay in Marketing / Free Gas

Lessor shall have the right to use up to 200,000 cubic feet of gas per year, free of charge, for one dwelling on the premises, at Lessor's own risk.

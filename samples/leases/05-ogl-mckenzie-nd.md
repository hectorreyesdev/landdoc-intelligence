# Paid-Up Oil and Gas Lease

Effective Date: May 5, 2024

Lessor: Arnold T. Bergstrom and Carol J. Bergstrom, as joint tenants

Lessee: Bakken Ridge Energy, Inc.

County: McKenzie, North Dakota


## Recitals

Lessor owns an interest in the oil, gas, and other minerals underlying the lands described herein and desires to lease the same to Lessee for development.

## Leased Lands

Township 150 North, Range 98 West, 5th P.M., Section 22: S/2, containing approximately 320.00 net mineral acres in McKenzie County, North Dakota.

## Consideration and Bonus

Lessee has paid Lessor $900.00 per net mineral acre as a paid-up bonus, the receipt of which is acknowledged, covering the full primary term with no delay rentals due.

## Term

Primary term of five (5) years, and so long thereafter as operations or production continue on the leased lands or lands pooled therewith.

## Royalty

18.75% (3/16) of production, delivered or paid free of the costs of exploration, drilling, and production.

## Depth and Continuous Operations

This lease covers all depths. A continuous-development clause requires Lessee to commence a new well within 180 days of completion of the prior well to hold acreage outside a producing unit.

## Execution

Executed by Arnold T. Bergstrom and Carol J. Bergstrom, as joint tenants (Lessor) and Bakken Ridge Energy, Inc. (Lessee) effective May 5, 2024.

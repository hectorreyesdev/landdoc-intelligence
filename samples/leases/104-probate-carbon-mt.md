IN THE PROBATE COURT

CARBON COUNTY, MONTANA


IN THE MATTER OF THE ESTATE OF ANDREW J. BIGELOW, DECEASED.

No. P-1104


# Order Admitting Will to Probate and Appointing Executor

On May 27, 2025 came on to be heard the application for probate of the written will of Andrew J. Bigelow, Deceased, and the Court, having heard the evidence, FINDS:

1. The Decedent died on December 5, 2024, and this Court has jurisdiction and venue. 2. The Decedent left a valid written will dated April 9, 2025, not revoked. 3. Carl Bigelow is named executor in the will and is not disqualified.

IT IS THEREFORE ORDERED that the will is admitted to probate; that Carl Bigelow is appointed and shall serve as independent executor without bond; and that letters testamentary issue upon qualification according to law.

The Decedent's estate includes, among other property, an interest in the oil, gas, and minerals described as Township 8 South, Range 20 East, M.P.M., Section 4: SE/4, Carbon County, Montana.


SIGNED this May 27, 2025.

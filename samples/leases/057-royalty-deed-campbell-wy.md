# Royalty Deed

THIS ROYALTY DEED is made August 30, 2024 by Ramon C. Trujillo ("Grantor") to Gulf Coast Minerals, LLC ("Grantee").

## Granting Clause

For valuable consideration of $385,000.00, Grantor grants and conveys unto Grantee a perpetual, non-participating royalty interest equal to a 1/32 of 8/8 of all oil, gas, and other minerals produced, saved, and sold from the land described below, free and clear of all costs of exploration, development, and production.

## Land

Township 50 North, Range 68 West, 6th P.M., Section 9: N/2, containing 320.00 acres, more or less, Campbell County, Wyoming.

## Non-Participating Nature

The interest conveyed is a non-participating royalty: Grantee shall not have the right to execute leases, to receive bonus or delay rentals, or to join in the making of oil and gas leases, all such executive rights being reserved to the mineral owner.

IN THE COUNTY COURT

LOVING COUNTY, TEXAS


IN THE MATTER OF THE ESTATE OF ANDREW J. HENDERSON, DECEASED.

No. P-1042


# Order Admitting Will to Probate and Appointing Executor

On May 6, 2019 came on to be heard the application for probate of the written will of Andrew J. Henderson, Deceased, and the Court, having heard the evidence, FINDS:

1. The Decedent died on March 2, 2019, and this Court has jurisdiction and venue. 2. The Decedent left a valid written will dated October 18, 2016, not revoked. 3. Carl A. Henderson is named executor in the will and is not disqualified.

IT IS THEREFORE ORDERED that the will is admitted to probate; that Carl A. Henderson is appointed and shall serve as independent executor without bond; and that letters testamentary issue upon qualification according to law.

The Decedent's estate includes, among other property, an interest in the oil, gas, and minerals described as Section 30, Block C-24, PSL Survey, Abstract No. 612, Loving County, Texas.


SIGNED this May 6, 2019.

# Drilling Title Opinion

TO: Mesa Verde Resources, LP

FROM: M. Castellano, Castellano Title Law PC

DATE: March 4, 2024

RE: BEGINNING at an iron pin at a corner of lands now or formerly of Buchanan; thence S 72 deg E 3749 feet to a post; thence S 8 deg W 4371 feet to a stone; thence N 79 deg W 3337 feet to a marked oak; thence N 7 deg E 4043 feet to the place of beginning, Fayette County, Pennsylvania (40.00 acres)


## A. Scope and Materials Examined

At your request, I have examined an abstract of title covering the captioned lands, comprising 271 numbered entries certified to January 12, 2024 by Permian Abstract & Title. This opinion is limited to record title and is rendered for the sole use of the addressee.

## B. Tract Ownership -- Mineral Estate

Tract 1 (the captioned lands). The mineral fee and executive right are owned by Clayton and Beatrice Monroe, subject to a non-participating royalty interest (NPRI) of 1/8 of 8/8 held by the Henderson Family. Total net mineral acres: 40.00.

## C. Schedule of Leases and Encumbrances

Lease 1: from the mineral owner to Mesa Verde Resources, LP, royalty 25% (1/4), yielding a net revenue interest to Lessee of 0.65625. Encumbrance: a deed of trust of record affecting the surface estate only, noted for information.

## D. Comments

Comment 1: The 1987 probate of the Estate of A. Henderson did not include a recorded order admitting the will; heirship is presumed from the family settlement agreement at Book 412, Page 88.

Comment 2: A prior oil and gas lease appears expired by its own terms for lack of production, but no release of record was located.

## E. Requirements

Requirement 1: Obtain and record a release of the expired lease referenced in Comment 2, or a stipulation of interest and non-development.

Requirement 2: Secure recorded affidavits of heirship for the Henderson interest before disbursing proceeds; suspend the corresponding decimal interest until cured.

Requirement 3: Confirm payment of ad valorem taxes for the current year; unpaid taxes constitute a first lien on the property.


Respectfully submitted,

M. Castellano, Castellano Title Law PC

# Paid-Up Oil and Gas Lease

Effective Date: March 1, 2025

Lessor: The Holloway Family Trust dated June 3, 1998

Lessee: Delaware Basin Resources, LP

County: Reeves, Texas


## Recitals

Lessor owns an interest in the oil, gas, and other minerals underlying the lands described herein and desires to lease the same to Lessee for development.

## Leased Lands

Section 22, Block 13, H&GN RR Co. Survey, Abstract No. 2204, containing approximately 320.00 net mineral acres in Reeves County, Texas.

## Consideration and Bonus

Lessee has paid Lessor $2,250.00 per net mineral acre as a paid-up bonus, the receipt of which is acknowledged, covering the full primary term with no delay rentals due.

## Term

Primary term of five (5) years, and so long thereafter as operations or production continue on the leased lands or lands pooled therewith.

## Royalty

22.5% (9/40) of production, delivered or paid free of the costs of exploration, drilling, and production.

## Depth and Continuous Operations

This lease covers all depths. A continuous-development clause requires Lessee to commence a new well within 180 days of completion of the prior well to hold acreage outside a producing unit.

## Execution

Executed by The Holloway Family Trust dated June 3, 1998 (Lessor) and Delaware Basin Resources, LP (Lessee) effective March 1, 2025.

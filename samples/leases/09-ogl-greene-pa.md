# Oil and Gas Lease (Appalachian Form)

This Lease, made and entered into on November 14, 2024, between Greene Hills Land Holdings, LLC, of Morgan Township, Greene County, Pennsylvania ("Lessor"), and Keystone Shale Partners, LP ("Lessee").


## Witnesseth

That for and in consideration of $2,500.00 per net acre and the covenants hereinafter contained, Lessor does hereby grant, demise, lease, and let unto Lessee, for the sole and only purpose of exploring for and producing oil and gas, all that certain tract of land described as follows:

## Description (Metes and Bounds)

BEGINNING at a fence post corner; thence along Township Road 388 N 71 deg E 980 feet; thence S 19 deg E 2,610 feet to a marked hickory; thence S 70 deg W 1,005 feet; thence N 18 deg W 2,640 feet to the point of beginning

Containing 61.80 acres, more or less, being the same premises described in Instrument No. 2016-004412, situate in Morgan Township, Greene County, Pennsylvania.

## Habendum / Term

To have and to hold for a term of five (5) years from the date hereof (the primary term) and so long thereafter as oil or gas is produced in paying quantities.

## Royalty

Lessee covenants to pay Lessor a royalty of fifteen percent (15%) of the net proceeds at the wellhead for all gas produced and marketed, and one-eighth (1/8) of all oil produced and saved from the leased premises.

## Free Gas

Lessor shall have the right to use up to 200,000 cubic feet of gas per year, free of charge, for one dwelling on the premises, at Lessor's own risk.

## Force Majeure

When operations or production are prevented or delayed by force majeure -- including acts of God, war, governmental regulation or delay in issuing permits, inability to obtain materials, or lack of available market or transportation -- this lease shall not terminate, the time of such delay shall not be counted against Lessee, and the lease shall be extended for so long as operations are so prevented or delayed.

# Amendment and Extension of Oil and Gas Lease

This Amendment and Extension ("Amendment") is made February 1, 2025 between James R. and Linda S. Whitaker, husband and wife ("Lessor") and Mesa Verde Resources, LP ("Lessee").

## Original Lease

Lessor and Lessee are parties to that certain Oil and Gas Lease dated February 10, 2022, recorded as 2022-0001998 in Book 880, Page 145 of the records of Lea County, New Mexico (the "Lease"), covering: Township 19 South, Range 38 East, N.M.P.M., Section 33: W/2, 320.00 acres.

## Extension of Primary Term

In consideration of $500.00 per net mineral acre, the primary term of the Lease is extended for an additional period of two (2) years from the expiration of the original primary term, upon the same terms except as amended below.

## Amended Royalty

The royalty under the Lease is amended and increased to one-fourth (1/4) of production, effective as to production on and after the date of this Amendment.

## Ratification

Except as expressly amended, all terms of the Lease are ratified and confirmed and remain in full force and effect. Lessor ratifies and confirms the Lease as amended.

# Release of Oil and Gas Lease

KNOW ALL PERSONS: Red River Minerals, LLC ("Releasor"), the present owner of the leasehold created by the oil and gas lease described below, for valuable consideration, hereby RELEASES, SURRENDERS, and QUITCLAIMS unto the present owner of the land all of Releasor's right, title, and interest in and to said lease.

## Lease Released

That certain Oil and Gas Lease dated October 17, 2024, from Clayton and Beatrice Monroe, as Lessor, recorded as Instrument No. 2023-0007625 in Book 1575, Page 1390 of the records of Harrison County, West Virginia, covering: BEGINNING at an iron pin at a corner of lands now or formerly of Buchanan; thence S 75 deg E 5525 feet to a post; thence S 11 deg W 5475 feet to a stone; thence N 85 deg W 4825 feet to a marked oak; thence N 7 deg E 4955 feet to the place of beginning.

Said lease is hereby terminated and held for naught, and the land is released from all obligations thereunder, effective as of the date below.

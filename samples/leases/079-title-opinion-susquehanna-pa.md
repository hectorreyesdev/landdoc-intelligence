# Drilling Title Opinion

TO: Front Range Petroleum, LLC

FROM: S. Nakamura, Nakamura Energy Law

DATE: August 30, 2024

RE: BEGINNING at an iron pin at a corner of lands now or formerly of Maple Run; thence S 74 deg E 3823 feet to a post; thence S 10 deg W 4417 feet to a stone; thence N 81 deg W 3399 feet to a marked oak; thence N 9 deg E 4081 feet to the place of beginning, Susquehanna County, Pennsylvania (160.00 acres)


## A. Scope and Materials Examined

At your request, I have examined an abstract of title covering the captioned lands, comprising 277 numbered entries certified to June 21, 2024 by Frontier Abstract Company. This opinion is limited to record title and is rendered for the sole use of the addressee.

## B. Tract Ownership -- Mineral Estate

Tract 1 (the captioned lands). The mineral fee and executive right are owned by Samuel O. Pruett, subject to a non-participating royalty interest (NPRI) of 1/8 of 8/8 held by the Henderson Family. Total net mineral acres: 160.00.

## C. Schedule of Leases and Encumbrances

Lease 1: from the mineral owner to Front Range Petroleum, LLC, royalty 25% (1/4), yielding a net revenue interest to Lessee of 0.65625. Encumbrance: a deed of trust of record affecting the surface estate only, noted for information.

## D. Comments

Comment 1: The 1987 probate of the Estate of A. Henderson did not include a recorded order admitting the will; heirship is presumed from the family settlement agreement at Book 412, Page 88.

Comment 2: A prior oil and gas lease appears expired by its own terms for lack of production, but no release of record was located.

## E. Requirements

Requirement 1: Obtain and record a release of the expired lease referenced in Comment 2, or a stipulation of interest and non-development.

Requirement 2: Secure recorded affidavits of heirship for the Henderson interest before disbursing proceeds; suspend the corresponding decimal interest until cured.

Requirement 3: Confirm payment of ad valorem taxes for the current year; unpaid taxes constitute a first lien on the property.

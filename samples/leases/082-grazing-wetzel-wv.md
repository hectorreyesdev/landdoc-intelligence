# Grazing and Ranch Lease

This Grazing Lease is made December 5, 2024 between Bigelow Family Partnership, LP ("Lessor") and Beartooth Land & Livestock ("Lessee").

## Leased Premises

Lessor leases to Lessee for grazing and ranching purposes the following lands: BEGINNING at an iron pin at a corner of lands now or formerly of Yoder; thence S 77 deg E 3934 feet to a post; thence S 4 deg W 4486 feet to a stone; thence N 84 deg W 3492 feet to a marked oak; thence N 4 deg E 4138 feet to the place of beginning, containing 320.00 acres, more or less, in Wetzel County, West Virginia (the "Ranch").

## Term and Rent

The term is two (2) years beginning December 5, 2024. Lessee shall pay annual rent of $18.00 per acre, payable annually in advance.

## Carrying Capacity

Stocking shall not exceed 2610 animal units; Lessee shall practice rotational grazing and shall not overgraze. Lessee maintains fences, gates, stock tanks, and windmills in good repair, ordinary wear excepted.

## Reservations

Lessor reserves all oil, gas, and mineral rights and the right to grant surface use to mineral lessees; Lessee's rent shall be equitably reduced for acreage taken out of grazing by such operations. Hunting rights are reserved to Lessor.

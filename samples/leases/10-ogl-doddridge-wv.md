# Oil and Gas Lease (Appalachian Form)

This Lease, made and entered into on August 19, 2024, between Floyd and Wanda Carpenter, husband and wife, of McClellan District, Doddridge County, West Virginia ("Lessor"), and Allegheny Gas Company ("Lessee").


## Witnesseth

That for and in consideration of $1,800.00 per net acre and the covenants hereinafter contained, Lessor does hereby grant, demise, lease, and let unto Lessee, for the sole and only purpose of exploring for and producing oil and gas, all that certain tract of land described as follows:

## Description (Metes and Bounds)

Beginning at a sugar maple on the bank of Middle Island Creek; thence with the creek N 44 deg E 62 poles; thence leaving the creek S 60 deg E 138 poles to a stone; thence S 40 deg W 70 poles; thence N 58 deg W 142 poles to the beginning

Containing 88.25 acres, more or less, being the same premises described in Deed Book 244, Page 19, situate in McClellan District, Doddridge County, West Virginia.

## Habendum / Term

To have and to hold for a term of five (5) years from the date hereof (the primary term) and so long thereafter as oil or gas is produced in paying quantities.

## Royalty

Lessee covenants to pay Lessor a royalty of one-eighth (1/8) of the net proceeds at the wellhead for all gas produced and marketed, and one-eighth (1/8) of all oil produced and saved from the leased premises.

## Delay in Marketing / Free Gas

Lessor shall have the right to use up to 200,000 cubic feet of gas per year, free of charge, for one dwelling on the premises, at Lessor's own risk.


WITNESS the hand and seal of Floyd and Wanda Carpenter, husband and wife, Lessor.

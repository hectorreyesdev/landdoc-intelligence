# Ratification of Oil and Gas Lease

The undersigned, Margaret A. Caldwell Mineral Trust ("Owner"), for valuable consideration, hereby RATIFIES, ADOPTS, and CONFIRMS that certain Oil and Gas Lease dated January 12, 2024, recorded as Instrument No. 2022-0007996 in Book 1624, Page 1467 of the records of Caddo County, Louisiana (the "Lease"), covering: Township 18 North, Range 14 West, Louisiana Meridian, Section 21: NE/4.

## Lease and Grant of Interest

Owner adopts the Lease as fully as if Owner had originally executed it, leases and lets Owner's interest in the described lands to the Lessee under the Lease upon its terms (royalty 18.75% (3/16)), and joins in and consents to the Lease's pooling and unitization provisions as to Owner's interest.

This Ratification is effective as to Owner's interest and does not impair the Lease as to any other interest.


EXECUTED June 21, 2024.

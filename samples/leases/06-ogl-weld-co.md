# Paid-Up Oil and Gas Lease

Effective Date: September 12, 2024

Lessor: Front Range Cattle Co., LLC

Lessee: Front Range Petroleum, LLC

County: Weld, Colorado


## Recitals

Lessor owns an interest in the oil, gas, and other minerals underlying the lands described herein and desires to lease the same to Lessee for development.

## Leased Lands

Township 6 North, Range 63 West, 6th P.M., Section 9: SW/4, containing approximately 160.00 net mineral acres in Weld County, Colorado.

## Consideration and Bonus

Lessee has paid Lessor $1,200.00 per net mineral acre as a paid-up bonus, the receipt of which is acknowledged, covering the full primary term with no delay rentals due.

## Term

Primary term of three (3) years, and so long thereafter as operations or production continue on the leased lands or lands pooled therewith.

## Royalty

20% (1/5) of production, delivered or paid free of the costs of exploration, drilling, and production.

## Depth and Continuous Operations

This lease covers all depths. A continuous-development clause requires Lessee to commence a new well within 180 days of completion of the prior well to hold acreage outside a producing unit.

## Execution

Executed by Front Range Cattle Co., LLC (Lessor) and Front Range Petroleum, LLC (Lessee) effective September 12, 2024.

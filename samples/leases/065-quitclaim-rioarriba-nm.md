# Quitclaim Deed

THIS QUITCLAIM DEED, executed June 21, 2024, by Esperanza Ranch Partners, Ltd. ("Grantor") to The Trujillo Family, LLC ("Grantee").

WITNESSETH, that Grantor, for and in consideration of $385,000.00, does hereby remise, release, and forever QUITCLAIM unto Grantee all of Grantor's right, title, and interest, if any, in and to the following described property:

## Property

A portion of the Mora Land Grant, Tract 3-A, as shown on the amended grant plat of record, containing 18.90 acres, more or less, situated in Rio Arriba County, New Mexico.

TO HAVE AND TO HOLD the same, together with all appurtenances, unto Grantee forever. This deed conveys only such interest as Grantor may hold and contains no warranty of title, express or implied.


GRANTOR: Esperanza Ranch Partners, Ltd.


STATE OF NEW MEXICO

COUNTY OF RIO ARRIBA

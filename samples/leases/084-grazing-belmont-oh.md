# Grazing and Ranch Lease

This Grazing Lease is made April 9, 2025 between Margaret A. Caldwell, a single woman ("Lessor") and Rock Creek Cattle Company ("Lessee").

## Leased Premises

Lessor leases to Lessee for grazing and ranching purposes the following lands: BEGINNING at an iron pin at a corner of lands now or formerly of Reynolds; thence S 79 deg E 4008 feet to a post; thence S 6 deg W 4532 feet to a stone; thence N 72 deg W 3554 feet to a marked oak; thence N 6 deg E 4176 feet to the place of beginning, containing 40.00 acres, more or less, in Belmont County, Ohio (the "Ranch").

## Term and Rent

The term is three (3) years beginning April 9, 2025. Lessee shall pay annual rent of $12.00 per acre, payable annually in advance.

## Carrying Capacity

Stocking shall not exceed 2670 animal units; Lessee shall practice rotational grazing and shall not overgraze. Lessee maintains fences, gates, stock tanks, and windmills in good repair, ordinary wear excepted.

## Reservations

Lessor reserves all oil, gas, and mineral rights and the right to grant surface use to mineral lessees; Lessee's rent shall be equitably reduced for acreage taken out of grazing by such operations. Hunting rights are reserved to Lessor.

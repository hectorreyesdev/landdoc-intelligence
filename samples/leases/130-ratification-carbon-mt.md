# Ratification of Oil and Gas Lease

The undersigned, Bigelow Family Partnership Mineral Trust ("Owner"), for valuable consideration, hereby RATIFIES, ADOPTS, and CONFIRMS that certain Oil and Gas Lease dated July 3, 2025, recorded as Instrument No. 2024-0007890 in Book 1610, Page 1445 of the records of Carbon County, Montana (the "Lease"), covering: Township 8 South, Range 20 East, M.P.M., Section 14: NW/4.

## Lease and Grant of Interest

Owner adopts the Lease as fully as if Owner had originally executed it, leases and lets Owner's interest in the described lands to the Lessee under the Lease upon its terms (royalty one-fifth (1/5)), and joins in and consents to the Lease's pooling and unitization provisions as to Owner's interest.

This Ratification is effective as to Owner's interest and does not impair the Lease as to any other interest.

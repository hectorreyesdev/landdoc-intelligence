# Grazing and Ranch Lease

This Grazing Lease is made February 14, 2025 between Doris and Walter Kestrel, as joint tenants ("Lessor") and Sweetwater Grazing Co. ("Lessee").

## Leased Premises

Lessor leases to Lessee for grazing and ranching purposes the following lands: BEGINNING at an iron pin at a corner of lands now or formerly of Buchanan; thence S 78 deg E 3971 feet to a post; thence S 5 deg W 4509 feet to a stone; thence N 85 deg W 3523 feet to a marked oak; thence N 5 deg E 4157 feet to the place of beginning, containing 640.00 acres, more or less, in Ritchie County, West Virginia (the "Ranch").

## Term and Rent

The term is three (3) years with a two-year option beginning February 14, 2025. Lessee shall pay annual rent of $22.00 per acre, payable annually in advance.

## Carrying Capacity

Stocking shall not exceed 2640 animal units; Lessee shall practice rotational grazing and shall not overgraze. Lessee maintains fences, gates, stock tanks, and windmills in good repair, ordinary wear excepted.

## Reservations

Lessor reserves all oil, gas, and mineral rights and the right to grant surface use to mineral lessees; Lessee's rent shall be equitably reduced for acreage taken out of grazing by such operations. Hunting rights are reserved to Lessor.


LESSOR: Doris and Walter Kestrel, as joint tenants

LESSEE: Sweetwater Grazing Co.

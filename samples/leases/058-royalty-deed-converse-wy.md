# Royalty Deed

THIS ROYALTY DEED is made October 17, 2024 by University Lands Heritage Trust ("Grantor") to Caleb and Marie Donnelly, as joint tenants ("Grantee").

## Granting Clause

For valuable consideration of Ten Dollars and other valuable consideration, Grantor grants and conveys unto Grantee a perpetual, non-participating royalty interest equal to one-half (1/2) of all oil, gas, and other minerals produced, saved, and sold from the land described below, free and clear of all costs of exploration, development, and production.

## Land

Township 32 North, Range 69 West, 6th P.M., Section 14: NW/4, containing 160.00 acres, more or less, Converse County, Wyoming.

## Non-Participating Nature

The interest conveyed is a non-participating royalty: Grantee shall not have the right to execute leases, to receive bonus or delay rentals, or to join in the making of oil and gas leases, all such executive rights being reserved to the mineral owner.

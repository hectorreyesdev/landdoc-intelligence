# Royalty Deed

THIS ROYALTY DEED is made May 30, 2025 by University Lands Heritage Trust ("Grantor") to Santa Rita Royalty Company ("Grantee").

## Granting Clause

For valuable consideration of $48,000.00, Grantor grants and conveys unto Grantee a perpetual, non-participating royalty interest equal to a 1/32 of 8/8 of all oil, gas, and other minerals produced, saved, and sold from the land described below, free and clear of all costs of exploration, development, and production.

## Land

Section 7, Block 2, H&TC RR Co. Survey, Abstract No. 89, containing 640.00 acres, more or less, Reagan County, Texas.

## Non-Participating Nature

The interest conveyed is a non-participating royalty: Grantee shall not have the right to execute leases, to receive bonus or delay rentals, or to join in the making of oil and gas leases, all such executive rights being reserved to the mineral owner.

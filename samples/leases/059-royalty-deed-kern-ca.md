# Royalty Deed

THIS ROYALTY DEED is made December 5, 2024 by Esperanza Ranch Partners, Ltd. ("Grantor") to Chisholm Trail Royalties, LLC ("Grantee").

## Granting Clause

For valuable consideration of $10.00 and other good and valuable consideration, Grantor grants and conveys unto Grantee a perpetual, non-participating royalty interest equal to one-fourth (1/4) of all oil, gas, and other minerals produced, saved, and sold from the land described below, free and clear of all costs of exploration, development, and production.

## Land

Township 29 South, Range 28 East, M.D.M., Section 16: SW/4, containing 160.00 acres, more or less, Kern County, California.

## Non-Participating Nature

The interest conveyed is a non-participating royalty: Grantee shall not have the right to execute leases, to receive bonus or delay rentals, or to join in the making of oil and gas leases, all such executive rights being reserved to the mineral owner.

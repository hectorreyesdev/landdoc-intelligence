# Amendment and Extension of Oil and Gas Lease

This Amendment and Extension ("Amendment") is made August 30, 2024 between Circle Bar Ranch, LLC ("Lessor") and Permian Basin Production Co. ("Lessee").

## Original Lease

Lessor and Lessee are parties to that certain Oil and Gas Lease dated September 15, 2025, recorded as 2025-0005611 in Book 1309, Page 972 of the records of Guernsey County, Ohio (the "Lease"), covering: BEGINNING at an iron pin at a corner of lands now or formerly of Coen; thence S 82 deg E 4119 feet to a post; thence S 9 deg W 4601 feet to a stone; thence N 75 deg W 3647 feet to a marked oak; thence N 9 deg E 4233 feet to the place of beginning, 120.50 acres.

## Extension of Primary Term

In consideration of $2,000.00 per net acre, the primary term of the Lease is extended for an additional period of three (3) years with a two-year option from the expiration of the original primary term, upon the same terms except as amended below.

## Amended Royalty

The royalty under the Lease is amended and increased to one-eighth (1/8) of production, effective as to production on and after the date of this Amendment.

## Ratification

Except as expressly amended, all terms of the Lease are ratified and confirmed and remain in full force and effect. Lessor ratifies and confirms the Lease as amended.

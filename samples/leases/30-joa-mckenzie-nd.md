# Joint Operating Agreement

(A.A.P.L. Form 610 -- 1989 Model Form Operating Agreement)


This Operating Agreement is dated April 15, 2024, by and among the parties who execute it ("Parties").

Operator: Bakken Ridge Energy, Inc..

Contract Area: Township 150 North, Range 98 West, 5th P.M., Section 22, McKenzie County, North Dakota.

## Parties and Working Interests

| Party | Working Interest |
| --- | --- |
| Bakken Ridge Energy, Inc. (Operator) | 60.00% |
| Missouri River Oil, LLC | 25.00% |
| Dakota Royalty & Working, LP | 15.00% |

## Article V -- Operator

Bakken Ridge Energy, Inc. is designated Operator and shall conduct operations as a reasonably prudent operator, but shall have no liability except for gross negligence or willful misconduct. Operator may be removed for cause by the affirmative vote of Non-Operators owning a majority of the working interest remaining after excluding Operator.

## Article VI -- Drilling and Development

The Initial Well shall be drilled to a depth sufficient to test the Bakken and Three Forks formations. Any Party may propose subsequent operations; a Party electing not to participate ("non-consent") relinquishes its share of production until the participating Parties recover 300% of such non-consenting Party's share of costs.

## Article VII -- Expenditures and Liability

Operator shall furnish an AFE for any single operation estimated to cost more than $50,000.00. The liability of the Parties is several, not joint or collective; each Party is responsible only for its proportionate share, secured by the lien and security interest granted in Article VII.B.

## Article XI / XV -- Force Majeure; Miscellaneous

Obligations (other than to make money payments) are suspended during force majeure. Exhibits A through G (including the Accounting Procedure and Insurance) are attached and incorporated by reference.


OPERATOR: Bakken Ridge Energy, Inc.

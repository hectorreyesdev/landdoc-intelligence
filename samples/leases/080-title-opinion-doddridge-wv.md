# Drilling Title Opinion

TO: Red River Minerals, LLC

FROM: T. Lindqvist, Attorney at Law, Lindqvist & Reyes PLLC

DATE: October 17, 2024

RE: BEGINNING at an iron pin at a corner of lands now or formerly of Hartzell; thence S 75 deg E 3860 feet to a post; thence S 11 deg W 4440 feet to a stone; thence N 82 deg W 3430 feet to a marked oak; thence N 2 deg E 4100 feet to the place of beginning, Doddridge County, West Virginia (120.50 acres)


## A. Scope and Materials Examined

At your request, I have examined an abstract of title covering the captioned lands, comprising 280 numbered entries certified to August 30, 2024 by Trans-Pecos Abstract Co.. This opinion is limited to record title and is rendered for the sole use of the addressee.

## B. Tract Ownership -- Mineral Estate

Tract 1 (the captioned lands). The mineral fee and executive right are owned by Nadine F. Holloway, subject to a non-participating royalty interest (NPRI) of 1/8 of 8/8 held by the Henderson Family. Total net mineral acres: 120.50.

## C. Schedule of Leases and Encumbrances

Lease 1: from the mineral owner to Red River Minerals, LLC, royalty 25% (1/4), yielding a net revenue interest to Lessee of 0.65625. Encumbrance: a deed of trust of record affecting the surface estate only, noted for information.

## D. Comments

Comment 1: The 1987 probate of the Estate of A. Henderson did not include a recorded order admitting the will; heirship is presumed from the family settlement agreement at Book 412, Page 88.

Comment 2: A prior oil and gas lease appears expired by its own terms for lack of production, but no release of record was located.

## E. Requirements

Requirement 1: Obtain and record a release of the expired lease referenced in Comment 2, or a stipulation of interest and non-development.

Requirement 2: Secure recorded affidavits of heirship for the Henderson interest before disbursing proceeds; suspend the corresponding decimal interest until cured.

Requirement 3: Confirm payment of ad valorem taxes for the current year; unpaid taxes constitute a first lien on the property.


Respectfully submitted,

T. Lindqvist, Attorney at Law, Lindqvist & Reyes PLLC

# Release of Oil and Gas Lease

KNOW ALL PERSONS: Keystone Shale Partners, LP ("Releasor"), the present owner of the leasehold created by the oil and gas lease described below, for valuable consideration, hereby RELEASES, SURRENDERS, and QUITCLAIMS unto the present owner of the land all of Releasor's right, title, and interest in and to said lease.

## Lease Released

That certain Oil and Gas Lease dated December 5, 2024, from The Reaves Living Trust, as Lessor, recorded as Instrument No. 2024-0007678 in Book 1582, Page 1401 of the records of Wetzel County, West Virginia, covering: BEGINNING at an iron pin at a corner of lands now or formerly of Reynolds; thence S 76 deg E 5562 feet to a post; thence S 3 deg W 5498 feet to a stone; thence N 72 deg W 4856 feet to a marked oak; thence N 8 deg E 4974 feet to the place of beginning.

Said lease is hereby terminated and held for naught, and the land is released from all obligations thereunder, effective as of the date below.


EXECUTED September 15, 2025.

# Release of Oil and Gas Lease

KNOW ALL PERSONS: Allegheny Gas Company ("Releasor"), the present owner of the leasehold created by the oil and gas lease described below, for valuable consideration, hereby RELEASES, SURRENDERS, and QUITCLAIMS unto the present owner of the land all of Releasor's right, title, and interest in and to said lease.

## Lease Released

That certain Oil and Gas Lease dated February 14, 2025, from Samuel O. Pruett, as Lessor, recorded as Instrument No. 2025-0007731 in Book 1589, Page 1412 of the records of Ritchie County, West Virginia, covering: BEGINNING at an iron pin at a corner of lands now or formerly of Maple Run; thence S 77 deg E 5599 feet to a post; thence S 4 deg W 5521 feet to a stone; thence N 73 deg W 4887 feet to a marked oak; thence N 9 deg E 4993 feet to the place of beginning.

Said lease is hereby terminated and held for naught, and the land is released from all obligations thereunder, effective as of the date below.


EXECUTED January 12, 2024.

RELEASOR: Allegheny Gas Company


STATE OF WEST VIRGINIA

COUNTY OF RITCHIE

# Right-of-Way and Pipeline Easement

This Right-of-Way and Easement Agreement is made August 4, 2024 by Cuero Creek Ranch, Ltd. ("Grantor") in favor of Guadalupe Midstream Partners, LP ("Grantee").

## Grant of Easement

For consideration of $60,300.00 ($150.00 per rod), Grantor grants Grantee a 50-foot wide easement and right-of-way to construct, operate, maintain, inspect, replace, and remove one or more pipelines for the transportation of oil, gas, water, and related substances, with appurtenant valves and cathodic protection.

## Location

The easement crosses the following land: out of the William Ponton Survey, Abstract No. 36, DeWitt County, Texas, the centerline being as staked and as shown on the plat attached as Exhibit A. The right-of-way traverses approximately 402 rods (4.20 acres of working space during construction).

## Depth and Restoration

Pipelines shall be buried a minimum of 48 inches below the surface and below the plow depth of cultivated land. Grantee shall restore the surface, repair fences and drainage, and pay for growing crops and timber actually damaged.

## Term

This easement shall continue so long as the right-of-way is used for the purposes granted; abandonment for twenty-four (24) consecutive months terminates the grant and title reverts to Grantor.

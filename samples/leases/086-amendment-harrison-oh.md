# Amendment and Extension of Oil and Gas Lease

This Amendment and Extension ("Amendment") is made June 21, 2024 between James R. and Linda S. Whitaker, husband and wife ("Lessor") and San Joaquin Oil Company ("Lessee").

## Original Lease

Lessor and Lessee are parties to that certain Oil and Gas Lease dated July 3, 2025, recorded as 2024-0005558 in Book 1302, Page 961 of the records of Harrison County, Ohio (the "Lease"), covering: BEGINNING at an iron pin at a corner of lands now or formerly of Hartzell; thence S 81 deg E 4082 feet to a post; thence S 8 deg W 4578 feet to a stone; thence N 74 deg W 3616 feet to a marked oak; thence N 8 deg E 4214 feet to the place of beginning, 160.00 acres.

## Extension of Primary Term

In consideration of $1,500.00 per net mineral acre, the primary term of the Lease is extended for an additional period of two (2) years from the expiration of the original primary term, upon the same terms except as amended below.

## Amended Royalty

The royalty under the Lease is amended and increased to one-sixth (1/6) of production, effective as to production on and after the date of this Amendment.

## Ratification

Except as expressly amended, all terms of the Lease are ratified and confirmed and remain in full force and effect. Lessor ratifies and confirms the Lease as amended.

# General Warranty Deed

THE STATE OF TEXAS

COUNTY OF MIDLAND


That Roaring Fork Holdings, LLC ("Grantor"), for and in consideration of the sum of $10.00 and other good and valuable consideration cash in hand paid by Caleb and Marie Donnelly, as joint tenants ("Grantee"), the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, has GRANTED, SOLD, and CONVEYED, and by these presents does GRANT, SELL, and CONVEY unto Grantee the following real property:

## Property

Section 7, Block 34, T&P RR Co. Survey, Abstract No. 89, containing 40.00 acres, more or less, situated in Midland County, Texas, together with all improvements thereon (the "Property").

## Reservations and Exceptions

Grantor RESERVES unto Grantor, Grantor's heirs and assigns, an undivided one-fourth (1/4) of all oil, gas, and other minerals in, on, and under the Property. This conveyance is subject to all easements, restrictions, and mineral reservations of record. (Note: where a grantor reserves a mineral fraction and warrants title, the Duhig rule may govern competing reservations.)

## Habendum and Warranty

TO HAVE AND TO HOLD the Property unto Grantee, Grantee's heirs, successors, and assigns forever; and Grantor binds Grantor and Grantor's heirs to WARRANT AND FOREVER DEFEND all and singular the Property unto Grantee against every person lawfully claiming the same.


EXECUTED on September 15, 2025.

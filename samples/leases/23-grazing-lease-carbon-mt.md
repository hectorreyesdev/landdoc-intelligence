# Grazing and Ranch Lease

This Grazing Lease is made March 1, 2025 between Beartooth Mountain Land Trust ("Lessor") and Rock Creek Cattle Company ("Lessee").

## Leased Premises

Lessor leases to Lessee for grazing and ranching purposes the following lands: Township 7 South, Range 20 East, M.P.M., Sections 4 and 9 (all), containing 1,280.00 acres, more or less, in Carbon County, Montana (the "Ranch").

## Term and Rent

The term is five (5) years beginning March 1, 2025. Lessee shall pay annual rent of $18.00 per acre, payable annually in advance on March 1.

## Carrying Capacity

Stocking shall not exceed 260 animal units; Lessee shall practice rotational grazing and shall not overgraze. Lessee maintains fences, gates, stock tanks, and windmills in good repair, ordinary wear excepted.

## Reservations

Lessor reserves all oil, gas, and mineral rights and the right to grant surface use to mineral lessees; Lessee's rent shall be equitably reduced for acreage taken out of grazing by such operations. Hunting rights are reserved to Lessor.

# Surface Use and Damage Agreement

This Surface Use and Damage Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on July 15, 2024 between Knutson Brothers Farm Partnership ("Surface Owner") and Bakken Ridge Energy, Inc. ("Operator").

## Recitals

WHEREAS Operator holds oil and gas leasehold rights beneath the lands of Surface Owner described as Township 146 North, Range 95 West, 5th P.M., Section 8: N/2, Dunn County, North Dakota (320.00 acres); and

WHEREAS the parties desire to set forth the terms governing Operator's use of the surface for drilling and production operations;

## Surface Damages

Operator shall pay Surface Owner $25,000.00 per well pad as initial surface damages, plus $30.00 per rod for new roads and pipelines installed across the property.

## Location of Operations

Well pads, tank batteries, and central facilities shall be located no closer than 500 feet to any existing residence or water well without the Surface Owner's written consent. Operator shall fence all production facilities and control noxious weeds.

## Reclamation

Upon plugging and abandonment, Operator shall remove all equipment, recontour the site to approximate original grade, replace topsoil, and reseed with a mix approved by Surface Owner within twelve (12) months.

## Water

Operator shall not use fresh water from Surface Owner's wells or ponds without separate written agreement and shall repair any damage to existing water sources.

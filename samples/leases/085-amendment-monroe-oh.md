# Amendment and Extension of Oil and Gas Lease

This Amendment and Extension ("Amendment") is made March 4, 2024 between The Holloway Family Trust dated June 3, 1998 ("Lessor") and Powder River Resources, LLC ("Lessee").

## Original Lease

Lessor and Lessee are parties to that certain Oil and Gas Lease dated May 27, 2025, recorded as 2023-0005505 in Book 1295, Page 950 of the records of Monroe County, Ohio (the "Lease"), covering: BEGINNING at an iron pin at a corner of lands now or formerly of Maple Run; thence S 80 deg E 4045 feet to a post; thence S 7 deg W 4555 feet to a stone; thence N 73 deg W 3585 feet to a marked oak; thence N 7 deg E 4195 feet to the place of beginning, 80.00 acres.

## Extension of Primary Term

In consideration of $1,000.00 per net mineral acre, the primary term of the Lease is extended for an additional period of five (5) years from the expiration of the original primary term, upon the same terms except as amended below.

## Amended Royalty

The royalty under the Lease is amended and increased to 22.5% (9/40) of production, effective as to production on and after the date of this Amendment.

## Ratification

Except as expressly amended, all terms of the Lease are ratified and confirmed and remain in full force and effect. Lessor ratifies and confirms the Lease as amended.

IN THE DISTRICT COURT

KERN COUNTY, CALIFORNIA


IN THE MATTER OF THE ESTATE OF ROY D. KESTREL, DECEASED.

No. P-1103


# Order Admitting Will to Probate and Appointing Executor

On April 9, 2025 came on to be heard the application for probate of the written will of Roy D. Kestrel, Deceased, and the Court, having heard the evidence, FINDS:

1. The Decedent died on October 17, 2024, and this Court has jurisdiction and venue. 2. The Decedent left a valid written will dated February 14, 2025, not revoked. 3. Carl Kestrel is named executor in the will and is not disqualified.

IT IS THEREFORE ORDERED that the will is admitted to probate; that Carl Kestrel is appointed and shall serve as independent executor without bond; and that letters testamentary issue upon qualification according to law.

The Decedent's estate includes, among other property, an interest in the oil, gas, and minerals described as Township 29 South, Range 28 East, M.D.M., Section 33, Kern County, California.


SIGNED this April 9, 2025.

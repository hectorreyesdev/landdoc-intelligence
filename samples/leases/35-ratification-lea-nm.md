# Ratification of Oil and Gas Lease

The undersigned, Whitaker Family Mineral Trust ("Owner"), for valuable consideration, hereby RATIFIES, ADOPTS, and CONFIRMS that certain Oil and Gas Lease dated February 10, 2025, recorded as Instrument No. 2025-0000922 in Book 1011, Page 77 of the records of Lea County, New Mexico (the "Lease"), covering: Township 20 South, Range 37 East, N.M.P.M., Section 16: SE/4.

## Lease and Grant of Interest

Owner adopts the Lease as fully as if Owner had originally executed it, leases and lets Owner's interest in the described lands to the Lessee under the Lease upon its terms (royalty three-sixteenths (3/16)), and joins in and consents to the Lease's pooling and unitization provisions as to Owner's interest.

This Ratification is effective as to Owner's interest and does not impair the Lease as to any other interest.


EXECUTED March 20, 2025.

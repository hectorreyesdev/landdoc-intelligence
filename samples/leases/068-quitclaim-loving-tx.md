# Quitclaim Deed

THIS QUITCLAIM DEED, executed December 5, 2024, by Pecos Valley Land Company ("Grantor") to Caleb and Marie Donnelly, as joint tenants ("Grantee").

WITNESSETH, that Grantor, for and in consideration of $48,000.00, does hereby remise, release, and forever QUITCLAIM unto Grantee all of Grantor's right, title, and interest, if any, in and to the following described property:

## Property

Section 18, Block 41, PSL Survey, Abstract No. 36, containing 320.00 acres, more or less, situated in Loving County, Texas.

TO HAVE AND TO HOLD the same, together with all appurtenances, unto Grantee forever. This deed conveys only such interest as Grantor may hold and contains no warranty of title, express or implied.


GRANTOR: Pecos Valley Land Company


STATE OF TEXAS

COUNTY OF LOVING

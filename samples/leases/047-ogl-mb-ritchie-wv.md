# Oil and Gas Lease (Appalachian Form)

This Lease, made and entered into on February 14, 2025, between The Holloway Family Trust dated June 3, 1998, of Clay District, Ritchie County, West Virginia ("Lessor"), and Bakken Ridge Energy, Inc. ("Lessee").


## Witnesseth

That for and in consideration of $350.00 per net mineral acre and the covenants hereinafter contained, Lessor does hereby grant, demise, lease, and let unto Lessee, for the sole and only purpose of exploring for and producing oil and gas, all that certain tract of land described as follows:

## Description (Metes and Bounds)

BEGINNING at an iron pin at a corner of lands now or formerly of Buchanan; thence S 72 deg E 2639 feet to a post; thence S 5 deg W 3681 feet to a stone; thence N 77 deg W 2407 feet to a marked oak; thence N 9 deg E 3473 feet to the place of beginning

Containing 320.00 acres, more or less, being the same premises described in Deed Book 512, Page 88, situate in Clay District, Ritchie County, West Virginia.

## Habendum / Term

To have and to hold for a term of three (3) years with a two-year option from the date hereof (the primary term) and so long thereafter as oil or gas is produced in paying quantities.

## Royalty

Lessee covenants to pay Lessor a royalty of one-sixth (1/6) of the net proceeds at the wellhead for all gas produced and marketed, and one-eighth (1/8) of all oil produced and saved from the leased premises.

## Free Gas

Lessor shall have the right to use up to 200,000 cubic feet of gas per year, free of charge, for one dwelling on the premises, at Lessor's own risk.

## Force Majeure

When operations or production are prevented or delayed by force majeure -- including acts of God, war, governmental regulation or delay in issuing permits, inability to obtain materials, or lack of available market or transportation -- this lease shall not terminate, the time of such delay shall not be counted against Lessee, and the lease shall be extended for so long as operations are so prevented or delayed.


WITNESS the hand and seal of The Holloway Family Trust dated June 3, 1998, Lessor.

IN THE COUNTY COURT

CONVERSE COUNTY, WYOMING


IN THE MATTER OF THE ESTATE OF ESTHER M. PRUETT, DECEASED.

No. P-1102


# Order Admitting Will to Probate and Appointing Executor

On February 14, 2025 came on to be heard the application for probate of the written will of Esther M. Pruett, Deceased, and the Court, having heard the evidence, FINDS:

1. The Decedent died on August 30, 2024, and this Court has jurisdiction and venue. 2. The Decedent left a valid written will dated December 5, 2024, not revoked. 3. Carl Pruett is named executor in the will and is not disqualified.

IT IS THEREFORE ORDERED that the will is admitted to probate; that Carl Pruett is appointed and shall serve as independent executor without bond; and that letters testamentary issue upon qualification according to law.

The Decedent's estate includes, among other property, an interest in the oil, gas, and minerals described as Township 32 North, Range 68 West, 6th P.M., Section 27: W/2, Converse County, Wyoming.


SIGNED this February 14, 2025.

# Assignment, Bill of Sale and Conveyance

This Assignment, Bill of Sale and Conveyance ("Assignment") is made effective April 9, 2025 (the "Effective Time"), from Delaware Basin Resources, LP ("Assignor") to Basin A&D Holdings, LLC ("Assignee").

## Granting Clause

For $48,000.00, Assignor GRANTS, BARGAINS, SELLS, ASSIGNS, and CONVEYS to Assignee all of Assignor's right, title, and interest in and to the oil and gas leases described on Exhibit A (the "Leases"), together with the lands covered, the wells, equipment, and the associated contracts and records (collectively, the "Assets").

The Assets are situated in Caddo Parish, Louisiana; the primary tract is described as Township 18 North, Range 14 West, Louisiana Meridian, Section 14: NW/4.

## Reserved Override

Assignor RESERVES an overriding royalty interest of 2.5% of 8/8ths in all production from the Leases, proportionately reduced to Assignor's net interest assigned.

# Assignment, Bill of Sale and Conveyance

This Assignment, Bill of Sale and Conveyance ("Assignment") is made effective May 27, 2025 (the "Effective Time"), from Mesa Verde Resources, LP ("Assignor") to Frontier Upstream, LP ("Assignee").

## Granting Clause

For $120,000.00, Assignor GRANTS, BARGAINS, SELLS, ASSIGNS, and CONVEYS to Assignee all of Assignor's right, title, and interest in and to the oil and gas leases described on Exhibit A (the "Leases"), together with the lands covered, the wells, equipment, and the associated contracts and records (collectively, the "Assets").

The Assets are situated in Midland County, Texas; the primary tract is described as Section 22, Block 2, H&TC RR Co. Survey, Abstract No. 1187.

## Reserved Override

Assignor RESERVES an overriding royalty interest of 3.0% of 8/8ths in all production from the Leases, proportionately reduced to Assignor's net interest assigned.

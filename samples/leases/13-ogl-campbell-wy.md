# Paid-Up Oil and Gas Lease

Effective Date: April 1, 2024

Lessor: Powder River Grazing Association

Lessee: Powder River Resources, LLC

County: Campbell, Wyoming


## Recitals

Lessor owns an interest in the oil, gas, and other minerals underlying the lands described herein and desires to lease the same to Lessee for development.

## Leased Lands

Township 49 North, Range 71 West, 6th P.M., Section 5: Lots 3 and 4, S/2 NW/4, containing approximately 158.40 net mineral acres in Campbell County, Wyoming.

## Consideration and Bonus

Lessee has paid Lessor $350.00 per net mineral acre as a paid-up bonus, the receipt of which is acknowledged, covering the full primary term with no delay rentals due.

## Term

Primary term of five (5) years, and so long thereafter as operations or production continue on the leased lands or lands pooled therewith.

## Royalty

one-sixth (1/6) of production, delivered or paid free of the costs of exploration, drilling, and production.

## Depth and Continuous Operations

This lease covers all depths. A continuous-development clause requires Lessee to commence a new well within 180 days of completion of the prior well to hold acreage outside a producing unit.

## Execution

Executed by Powder River Grazing Association (Lessor) and Powder River Resources, LLC (Lessee) effective April 1, 2024.

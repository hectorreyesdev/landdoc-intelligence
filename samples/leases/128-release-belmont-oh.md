# Release of Oil and Gas Lease

KNOW ALL PERSONS: Buckeye Utica Operating, LLC ("Releasor"), the present owner of the leasehold created by the oil and gas lease described below, for valuable consideration, hereby RELEASES, SURRENDERS, and QUITCLAIMS unto the present owner of the land all of Releasor's right, title, and interest in and to said lease.

## Lease Released

That certain Oil and Gas Lease dated April 9, 2025, from Nadine F. Holloway, as Lessor, recorded as Instrument No. 2022-0007784 in Book 1596, Page 1423 of the records of Belmont County, Ohio, covering: BEGINNING at an iron pin at a corner of lands now or formerly of Hartzell; thence S 78 deg E 5636 feet to a post; thence S 5 deg W 5544 feet to a stone; thence N 74 deg W 4918 feet to a marked oak; thence N 2 deg E 5012 feet to the place of beginning.

Said lease is hereby terminated and held for naught, and the land is released from all obligations thereunder, effective as of the date below.

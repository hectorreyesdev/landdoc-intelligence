# Assignment, Bill of Sale and Conveyance

This Assignment, Bill of Sale and Conveyance ("Assignment") is made effective July 1, 2025 (the "Effective Time"), from Llano Estacado Operating, LLC ("Assignor") to Permian Acquisition Partners, LP ("Assignee").

## Granting Clause

For $10.00 and other good and valuable consideration, Assignor GRANTS, BARGAINS, SELLS, ASSIGNS, and CONVEYS to Assignee all of Assignor's right, title, and interest in and to the oil and gas leases described on Exhibit A (the "Leases"), together with the lands covered, the wells, equipment, and the associated contracts and records (collectively, the "Assets").

## Reserved Override

Assignor RESERVES an overriding royalty interest of 2.0% of 8/8ths in all production from the Leases, proportionately reduced to Assignor's net interest assigned.

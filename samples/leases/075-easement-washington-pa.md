# Right-of-Way and Pipeline Easement

This Right-of-Way and Easement Agreement is made July 3, 2025 by University Lands Heritage Trust ("Grantor") in favor of Guadalupe Midstream Partners, LP ("Grantee").

## Grant of Easement

For consideration of $45,000.00, Grantor grants Grantee a permanent easement 30 feet in width to construct, operate, maintain, inspect, replace, and remove one pipeline for the transportation of oil, gas, water, and related substances, with appurtenant valves and cathodic protection, together with a temporary construction easement 50 feet in width during initial installation only.

## Location

The easement crosses the following land: BEGINNING at an iron pin at a corner of lands now or formerly of Coen; thence S 70 deg E 3675 feet to a post; thence S 6 deg W 4325 feet to a stone; thence N 77 deg W 3275 feet to a marked oak; thence N 5 deg E 4005 feet to the place of beginning, Washington County, Pennsylvania, the centerline being as staked and as shown on the plat attached as Exhibit A. The right-of-way traverses approximately 1575 rods (3.50 acres of permanent working space).

## Depth, Double-Ditching, and Restoration

The pipeline shall be buried a minimum of 48 inches below the surface and below the plow depth of cultivated land. Grantee shall double-ditch so that topsoil is separated and returned to the surface, repair fences, drainage, and terraces, re-seed annually until vegetation is re-established, and pay for growing crops and timber actually damaged.

## One Pipeline; Term and Abandonment

This grant is limited to a single pipeline; any additional line requires separate consideration. The easement continues so long as used for the purposes granted; abandonment for twenty-four (24) consecutive months terminates the grant and title reverts to Grantor.

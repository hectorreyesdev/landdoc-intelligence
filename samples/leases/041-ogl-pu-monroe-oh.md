# Paid-Up Oil and Gas Lease

Effective Date: September 15, 2025

Lessor: The Reaves Living Trust

Lessee: San Joaquin Oil Company

County: Monroe, Ohio


## Recitals

Lessor owns an interest in the oil, gas, and other minerals underlying the lands described herein and desires to lease the same to Lessee for development.

## Leased Lands

BEGINNING at an iron pin at a corner of lands now or formerly of Buchanan; thence S 81 deg E 2417 feet to a post; thence S 8 deg W 3543 feet to a stone; thence N 85 deg W 2221 feet to a marked oak; thence N 3 deg E 3359 feet to the place of beginning, containing approximately 640.00 net mineral acres in Monroe County, Ohio.

This lease also covers and includes all land owned or claimed by Lessor adjacent or contiguous to the land particularly described above, whether the same be in said survey or in adjacent surveys, although not included within the boundaries above (the "Mother Hubbard" clause).

## Consideration and Bonus

Lessee has paid Lessor $500.00 per net mineral acre as a paid-up bonus, the receipt of which is acknowledged, covering the full primary term with no delay rentals due.

## Term

Primary term of two (2) years, and so long thereafter as operations or production continue on the leased lands or lands pooled therewith.

## Royalty

one-fifth (1/5) of production, delivered or paid free of the costs of exploration, drilling, and production.

## Continuous Development

A continuous-development clause requires Lessee to commence a new well within 180 days of completion of the prior well to hold acreage outside a producing unit; this lease covers all depths.

## Force Majeure

When operations or production are prevented or delayed by force majeure -- including acts of God, war, governmental regulation or delay in issuing permits, inability to obtain materials, or lack of available market or transportation -- this lease shall not terminate, the time of such delay shall not be counted against Lessee, and the lease shall be extended for so long as operations are so prevented or delayed.

## Pugh Clause

Pugh Clause: Upon expiration of the primary term, this lease shall terminate as to all lands and all depths not then included within a producing or pooled unit, unless Lessee is then conducting continuous operations as provided herein.

## Execution

Executed by The Reaves Living Trust (Lessor) and San Joaquin Oil Company (Lessee) effective September 15, 2025.

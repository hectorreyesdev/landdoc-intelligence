# General Warranty Deed

THE STATE OF LOUISIANA

COUNTY OF CADDO


That Dorothy M. Albright, a widow ("Grantor"), for and in consideration of the sum of Ten Dollars and other valuable consideration cash in hand paid by Gulf Coast Minerals, LLC ("Grantee"), the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, has GRANTED, SOLD, and CONVEYED, and by these presents does GRANT, SELL, and CONVEY unto Grantee the following real property:

## Property

Township 18 North, Range 14 West, Louisiana Meridian, Section 27: W/2, containing 320.00 acres, more or less, situated in Caddo County, Louisiana, together with all improvements thereon (the "Property").

## Reservations and Exceptions

Grantor RESERVES unto Grantor, Grantor's heirs and assigns, an undivided one-half (1/2) of all oil, gas, and other minerals in, on, and under the Property. This conveyance is subject to all easements, restrictions, and mineral reservations of record. (Note: where a grantor reserves a mineral fraction and warrants title, the Duhig rule may govern competing reservations.)

## Habendum and Warranty

TO HAVE AND TO HOLD the Property unto Grantee, Grantee's heirs, successors, and assigns forever; and Grantor binds Grantor and Grantor's heirs to WARRANT AND FOREVER DEFEND all and singular the Property unto Grantee against every person lawfully claiming the same.


EXECUTED on July 3, 2025.

GRANTOR: Dorothy M. Albright, a widow
